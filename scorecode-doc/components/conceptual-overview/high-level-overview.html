High-level overview

CCCEM stands for Cookie Clicker Combo Execution Mod, with the goal of enabling easy practice of all kinds of combos.
Practice needs feedback. While you can model your combo scoring based on just how many cookies you get, in bigger combos this amount is heavily influenced by luck. Sometimes you would get more cookies, just because you got more buffs by chance - this is the problem that the Combo scoring system is designed to solve, outputting a score based on your displayed skill instead of luck.

Before CCCEM v3.5, the scoring system is entirely hardcoded and there was no practical method to adapt the scoring formula for all combos. Version 3.5 attempts to solve this problem by breaking down and opening up the "black box" of the scoring system, turning it into a modular system that anyone can modify; this has the main benefit of allowing users to adjust their own practice goals, and refine their skills in a way that is most useful for them.

This system is powered by a custom programming language called "Scorecode", harnessed by three main components: Watchers, Trackers, and Stats. The Scorecode language itself is similar to lambda calculus. More details below.

Score evaluation

To analyze the execution, the system keeps information about the game and analyzes them through the following three sections:

  1. Watchers: Watchers constantly gather information from the game. Each Watcher gather a different, specific type of information. There is a fixed set of Watchers and they cannot be added, removed, or modified.
  2. Trackers: Trackers are the main building blocks of the system, keeping track of important information. Each Tracker can hold one number that they can update at times defined by its settings. How a Tracker updates its stored value is defined by its Scorecode formula, which pulls information from Watchers or other Trackers.
  3. Stats: Stats are the final output of the system, and they are calculated based on the Trackers' values. An example of a stat is the Score, which is displayed at the end of each combo attempt, but other kinds of stats exist. They combine information from trackers to calculate its value when the player clicks "Try again", also defined by its Scorecode formula.

Information about the Scorecode language can also be found in this documentation site.

Editing the system

You can easily edit Trackers or Stats via the corresponding buttons in the Scoring & Evaluation section in the CCCEM Interface, provided that you aren't locked in a preset (click "Edit preset" if it is present) and that Advanced mode is turned ON.
These settings are saved and bundled to your current settings; exporting your settings will also export them, and the same goes for importing.
