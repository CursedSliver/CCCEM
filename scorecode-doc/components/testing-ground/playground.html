Playground

A limited testing environment for Scorecode. Type a formula, hit Evaluate, and the result is shown below. The mock Watchers panel on the right gives you a handful of stand-in Watchers with varying arities you can call to confirm that argument passing works the way you expect.

Ctrl+Enter inside the formula box to evaluate.

Mock Watchers

Each entry is a stand-in for a real CCCEM watcher. Click Insert to paste its call (with default args) into the formula box, then Evaluate.
